Delete your Rakt Pravah account

Rakt Pravah · an initiative of Vidya Bharati Purv Chatra Parishad

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rakt Pravah and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email alok020505@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with. Use the subject "Delete my account" and include the phone number on the account. We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • your profile: name, phone number, blood group, date of birth, location and photo;
  • your donation history and devices;
  • your sign-in. Your email and phone number can then be used for a new account.

Also when you delete your account:

  • Your open blood requests are cancelled.
  • Your name, photo and phone number are removed from all your requests.
  • Requests you accepted but hadn't finished go back to searching for another donor.

What is kept

  • Chat messages you sent stay with the person you sent them to.
  • A completed donation stays on the request it was made for, as the patient's record.
