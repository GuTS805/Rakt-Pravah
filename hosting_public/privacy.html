Privacy policy

Rakt Pravah · Effective 2 November 2026

Rakt Pravah is an app that connects people who need blood with volunteer donors nearby. It is an initiative of Vidya Bharati Purv Chatra Parishad ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we never see it.
  • Profile: your name, phone number, blood group, date of birth, gender, and your country, state and city. You can also add a profile photo if you want to.
  • Phone verification: we send a one-time code by SMS to confirm your phone number belongs to you.
  • Location:
    • With your permission, your device's location is used to match you with requests and donors nearby.
    • While you are on the way to donate, and only if you choose to share it, your live location is sent to the person who asked for blood.
  • Blood requests you make: the patient's name and blood group, the hospital, the number of units, a contact phone number and any notes.
  • Donations: requests you accepted, your donations and the date of your last donation. The date is used for the 90-day gap between donations.
  • Messages: chat messages between you and the person you are coordinating a donation with.
  • Reports: problems or misuse you report to us.
  • Device: a notification token so we can alert you about requests, and a device identifier.

We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for anything other than running Rakt Pravah.

Who can see what

  • Your profile is private. Only you and the Rakt Pravah team can see it.
  • A blood request can be seen by signed-in users, so that matching donors can find it. This includes the requester's name, the blood group and the hospital. The patient's name is shown only to the requester and the donors who accepted.
  • When a donor accepts a request, the donor and the requester can see each other's name and phone number, chat, and see live location if it is being shared.
  • Live location sharing stops automatically when the request is completed or cancelled.

Services we use

The app runs on Google Firebase. Accounts and phone verification use Firebase Authentication, data is stored in Cloud Firestore, notifications are sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and this website uses Firebase Hosting. Google processes this data for us, and it may be stored on Google's servers outside India. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete:

  • your profile, phone number, donation history and devices;
  • your sign-in.

Also when you delete your account:

  • Your open requests are cancelled.
  • Your name, photo and phone number are removed from all your requests.
  • Requests you accepted but hadn't finished go back to searching for another donor.
  • What stays: chat messages you sent stay with the person you sent them to. A completed donation stays on the request it was made for.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what data we hold about you;
  • correct it (you can also edit your profile in the app);
  • have it erased;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • raise a grievance.

You can delete your account yourself in the app, under You, then Delete account; see how to delete your account. For anything else, email us.

Age

Rakt Pravah is meant for adults. You need to be 18 or older to use it, and donors must be between 18 and 65.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact and grievances
