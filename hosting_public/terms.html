Rakt Pravah · Effective 2 November 2026

Rakt Pravah is an initiative of Vidya Bharati Purv Chatra Parishad. It connects people who need blood with volunteer donors. By using the app, you agree to these terms.

What Rakt Pravah is, and isn't

  • We put people in touch. We are not a hospital, blood bank or medical service, and we don't give medical advice.
  • The hospital or blood bank decides. Whether someone can donate, and every transfusion, is decided by them after their own screening and tests.
  • Donors are volunteers. We can't promise that a donor will be found, or that one will arrive in time.
  • In an emergency, contact a hospital or call 112 as well as using the app.

Your responsibilities

  • Give true information about yourself and about any request you make.
  • Only make a request for a real, current need, and close it when it is met.
  • Never ask for, offer or accept money for blood. Selling blood is illegal in India.
  • Use the phone numbers, locations and messages you see only to arrange the donation. Don't share them or use them for anything else.
  • Be respectful. Don't harass, threaten or mislead anyone.
  • As a donor, follow the 90-day gap between donations. Only donate if you are well and eligible.

Misuse

You can report a problem from the app. We may switch off accounts that break these terms or put others at risk.

Liability

Rakt Pravah is offered free, as it is. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for:

  • whether a donation takes place;
  • its outcome;
  • what users do or say;
  • the app not being available.

How we handle your data is explained in our privacy policy. You can delete your account at any time; see how.
